01 // Services

What We Do

Four focused services for individuals and small businesses that need their systems secure, their software working, and their hardware back in service.

01

Cybersecurity & Hardening

Security reviews and hardening for workstations, servers, and small networks: updates and patching, configuration lockdown, firewall and VPN setup, and access control, followed by a clear written summary of what changed.

System Hardening Firewall & VPN Security Review
02

Software & Embedded Systems

Custom scripts, automation, and embedded software for Linux machines and microcontroller-based devices, including single-board computers, sensor integration over I2C, SPI, and UART, and connecting devices to the services you already use.

Linux & Automation Microcontrollers I2C / SPI / UART
03

Hardware Diagnostics & Repair

Component-level board diagnostics to find the part that actually failed, micro-soldering and rework to replace it, and custom workstation builds specified for the work you need them to do.

Board Diagnostics Micro-Soldering Custom Builds
04

Firmware & SPI Flash Recovery

Reading, dumping, and reflashing SPI flash chips; BIOS/UEFI recovery for boards that won't boot after a failed update; EEPROM reads and writes; and firmware backups verified against the original.

SPI Flash BIOS / UEFI EEPROM Backup & Verify

03 // Process

How a Project Works

A simple, three-step process so you always know where your project stands.

  1. STEP 01

    Diagnose

    We start by understanding the problem or goal, then inspect, test, and measure to pin down the cause and agree on a plan before any work begins.

  2. STEP 02

    Fix or Build

    We carry out the repair, hardening, firmware recovery, or build, then test the result to confirm it works as expected.

  3. STEP 03

    Hand Off & Document

    You get your system back along with notes on what was done, any backups or firmware images, and recommendations for keeping it running.
